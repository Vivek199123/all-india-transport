Move packaged FMCG consignments from Vadodara, Ahmedabad, Surat and other Gujarat business centres to distributors, warehouses and commercial destinations across India.

Suitable cargo: packaged foods, beverages, household products, personal-care goods, cartons and other non-perishable consumer products. Cargo acceptance depends on shipment details and applicable transport requirements.

FTL and PTL options

Choose FTL when you need a dedicated vehicle for larger or time-sensitive consignments. PTL can suit smaller commercial loads that do not require an entire truck. We review volume, weight, route and handling needs before suggesting an option.

Information needed for planning

  • Pickup and delivery locations
  • Number of cartons or pallets
  • Total weight and approximate dimensions
  • Product and packaging description
  • Dispatch date and delivery expectations

Why use a Gujarat-based transport partner?

Local coordination helps manufacturers and distributors plan pickups from Gujarat industrial areas while connecting to major interstate routes. All India Transport provides a single point of contact for the road-freight movement.
