Plastic & Polymer Transportation in Gujarat

All India Transport coordinates interstate road movement for polymer granules, resins, plastic components, finished products and packaging inputs from Gujarat.

Shipment planning: Provide the exact material description, packing type, number of bags or pallets, weight and dimensions. Regulated or restricted materials require separate review and supporting documents.

Cargo we can assess

  • Polymer and plastic granules in sealed bags
  • Finished moulded or extruded plastic products
  • Industrial plastic components
  • Packaging films, containers and related inputs
  • Non-hazardous resins with correct documentation

Vehicle and load options

FTL may suit bulk bagged or palletised consignments that need a dedicated vehicle. PTL can work for smaller loads where consolidation is practical. Vehicle selection depends on cargo volume, stackability, packaging and route.

Gujarat pickup coverage

Transport enquiries can be coordinated from Vadodara, Ahmedabad, Surat, Vapi, Rajkot and other industrial locations, subject to vehicle availability and shipment requirements.
