Service Types

TYPES OF TRUCKING SERVICES

📦
Less-than-Truckload (LTL)

Transport of small shipments that don't require a full truck — cost-effective and efficient for smaller businesses.

🚛
Full Truckload (FTL)

Dedicated truck for large shipments — faster, more secure, and ideal for bulk cargo requiring exclusive transport.

🏗️
Flatbed Trucking

Transport of oversized or heavy items using specialized flatbed trailers — machinery, steel, construction materials.

❄️
Refrigerated Trucking

Temperature-controlled transport for perishable goods — food, pharmaceuticals, chemicals requiring cold chain.

⚠️
Hazardous Materials

Safe transport of dangerous goods with special handling, certified drivers, and compliance with all regulations.

⚡
Express Trucking

Fast delivery for time-sensitive or urgent shipments — priority routing and dedicated express lanes.
